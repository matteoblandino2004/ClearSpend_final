Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026
In short: All of your data stays on your own device. ClearSpend never collects, transmits, or sees any of it.

What data the app stores

When you use ClearSpend, you may enter information such as your income, monthly commitments, credit card details, purchases, budgets, and savings goals. All of this information is stored locally on your own device using your device's built-in storage.

What we collect

Nothing. ClearSpend does not have user accounts, does not connect to any server, and does not transmit your data anywhere. The developer has no access to any information you enter. No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

Sharing your data

Because your data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share with anyone. No third parties receive your information.

Deleting your data

Your data is stored only in your device's app storage. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by using any in-app reset option provided.

Children's privacy

ClearSpend is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them (and in fact collects no information from anyone).
