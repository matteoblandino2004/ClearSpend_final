Interest Calculator

When you don't pay off your full credit card balance, the bank charges you extra money called interest — basically a fee for borrowing. This tool shows you in plain dollars how much that fee is costing you, and how long it'll take to become debt-free.
New to this? Here's the short version:
A credit card lets you borrow money to buy things. If you pay back the full amount each month, it costs you nothing extra. But if you only pay part of it, the bank charges interest — a daily fee on whatever you still owe. The fee is based on your card's APR (the yearly interest rate, usually around 20–30%). Fill in the boxes below and we'll show you exactly what it's costing you in real dollars.
Your card details
Don't worry if you don't know every number — just fill in what you can. Your APR and balance are printed on your monthly statement or in your card's app.
